# Charter of the ROS Project Working Group "Client Libraries"

4 September 2026

### Names

The names of the Working Group shall be "ROS Working Group on Client Libraries", "Client Library WG" and "ROS-WG-CLIENT-LIBRARIES".

### Chair

The Chair of the Working Group shall be [Alberto Soragna](mailto:alberto.soragna@gmail.com).

### Purpose

The Working Group is formed to design, develop, and maintain the ROS 2 client libraries and their supporting libraries, which provide the primary user-facing APIs of ROS 2. The Working Group's scope includes:

- the foundational client library, rcl, and the core client libraries rclcpp and rclpy;
- the examples and demos that illustrate correct use of the client library APIs.

The Working Group has been active in the ROS community since before the establishment of the OSRA, holding regular open meetings announced on the OSRF Discourse and listed on the official ROS events calendar. This Charter formalises the Working Group under the governance of the ROS Project.

### Tasks

The Working Group shall perform the following tasks.

1. Triage issues and review pull requests for the repositories under the Working Group's scope, with the goal of resolving discussions and moving contributions towards acceptance or rejection.
2. Discuss and design new features and improvements for the client libraries, and identify usability improvements needed by users of the client library APIs.
3. Provide a regular open forum in which contributors and users of the client libraries can raise and discuss design questions, controversial changes, and priorities.

### Expected outputs

The Working Group shall, upon completion of its tasks and as appropriate while performing them, provide to the ROS PMC the following.

1. Bug fixes, usability improvements, new features, and design documentation for the client libraries.
2. Recommendations to the ROS PMC on the direction and priorities of client library development, when requested or when the Working Group deems it necessary.

### Completion date

The Working Group's Charter expires on the 4th September 2027.
The Working Group shall be automatically dissolved on that date, unless its Charter is explicitly renewed by the ROS PMC.

### Meetings

The Working Group shall meet at least every 2 weeks.
Meetings shall be noticed at least three (3) calendar days in advance to the ROS PMC Members and Committers.
Meeting notice shall include the date, time, and URL of the online meeting.
A single announcement noticing a series of meetings together shall be considered sufficient notice for all meetings in the series, provided that notice of the cancellation of one of those meetings shall be given at least one (1) calendar day in advance.

Meetings shall be held online via a suitable teleconference service as specified by the ROS PMC.

### Activity reports

The Chair of the Working Group shall provide a verbal report of its recent activities in each meeting of the ROS PMC.
The Chair shall additionally provide the ROS PMC with any documents generated by the activities of the Working Group, if and when requested by the ROS PMC to do so.
